Suzuki GSR600
Manufacturer Suzuki
Production 2006–2011
Displacement 599 cm³
Power 72 kW

The Suzuki GSR600 is a naked motorcycle produced by Suzuki between 2006 and 2011. It uses a detuned version of the GSX-R600 inline-four engine in a steel tube frame, and was sold in Europe as an entry into the middleweight naked class.[1]

Contemporary reviews described the GSR600 as a competent all-rounder whose steel frame and upright riding position made it approachable for riders moving up from a 500, while its supersport-derived engine kept it entertaining well past the legal limit on a back road.

Design

The 599 cm³ engine delivers 72 kW at 12,000 rpm and 63 N⋅m of torque. The riding position is upright, with a seat height of 785 mm and a wet weight of 205 kg. Suzuki retuned the camshafts and the intake tract for mid-range response rather than peak power, which is why the naked bike gives away roughly twenty kilowatts to the supersport it descends from.

Chassis and brakes

Twin 290 mm front discs with two-piston calipers were fitted throughout production; ABS was never offered on this model. The rear shock is adjustable for preload only, and the fork is non-adjustable, a cost decision that reviewers of the period regularly singled out.

Engine

The inline-four is the GSX-R600 K5 unit with revised cams, a heavier crankshaft and a shorter intake, mapped for a flatter torque curve. Service intervals are every 6,000 km, and the engine is widely regarded as durable well beyond 60,000 km when the valve clearances are kept in specification.

Reception

Reviewers praised the engine and the price and criticised the suspension and the lack of ABS. The GSR600 never sold in the numbers of its Japanese rivals, and it was replaced in 2011 by the GSR750, which used a larger detuned supersport engine in a similar chassis.
