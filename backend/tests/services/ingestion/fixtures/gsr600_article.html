Suzuki GSR600

The Suzuki GSR600 is a naked motorcycle produced by Suzuki between 2006 and 2011. It uses a detuned version of the GSX-R600 inline-four engine in a steel tube frame, and was sold in Europe as an entry into the middleweight naked class.

Design

The 599 cm³ engine delivers 72 kW at 12,000 rpm and 63 Nm of torque. The riding position is upright, with a seat height of 785 mm and a wet weight of 205 kg.

Chassis and brakes

Twin 290 mm front discs with two-piston calipers were fitted throughout production; ABS was never offered on this model.

Reception

Reviewers praised the engine's flexibility and criticised the soft suspension. Later comparisons place it between the Yamaha FZ6 and the Honda Hornet 600, and note that the model is frequently recommended as a first middleweight.
