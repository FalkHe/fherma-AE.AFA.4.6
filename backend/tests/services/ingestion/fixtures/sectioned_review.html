Riding impressions

The GSR600 feels lighter than its 205 kg wet weight suggests once it is rolling, and the detuned supersport engine pulls cleanly from 4,000 rpm all the way to the limiter without the peaky delivery of the GSX-R600 it borrows from.

Living with it

Over 18,000 km the only unscheduled work was a rear brake caliper rebuild. Fuel consumption settled at 5.4 l/100 km in mixed use, and the chain needed adjustment every 1,500 km, which is about average for a naked middleweight of this era.

What we would change

The non-adjustable fork is the weak point: it dives under hard braking and there is no way to dial that out without aftermarket internals. A taller screen also makes the motorway stretch considerably less tiring at 120 km/h.
